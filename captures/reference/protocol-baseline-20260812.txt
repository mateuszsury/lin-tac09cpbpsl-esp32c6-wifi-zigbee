Klima WiFi protocol baseline, 2026-08-12

Hardware:
  display TD-YD-PSL-XS V1.0 / 810901115C
  main controller 810901123B / D10B

Electrical: 3.3 V UART, idle high
Serial: 9600 baud, 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit
Cadence: approximately 100 ms in each direction

No-jumper MITM wiring:
  panel TX -> GPIO5 (panel-side RX)
  GPIO4 -> panel RX (panel-side TX)
  main TX -> GPIO6 (main-side RX)
  GPIO7 -> main RX (main-side TX)

Temporary passive bypass wiring (currently installed):
  GPIO4 <-> GPIO6
  GPIO5 <-> GPIO7
  passive firmware listens on GPIO4 and GPIO7; every link GPIO is input-only

Panel -> main, complete OFF/18 C frame, 15 bytes:
  54 44 0B 11 31 00 12 00 00 40 00 00 00 00 79

Main -> panel, complete OFF/18 C frame, 22 bytes:
  54 44 12 11 31 00 12 00 00 00 00 2C 2C 00 40 0A 00 00 00 00 00 6A

For both complete frame types XOR of every byte equals 0x10. The last byte is
therefore generated as XOR(all preceding bytes) XOR 0x10.

Panel field correlations (zero-based indexes):
  [0..1]  54 44 (ASCII TD header)
  [2]     0B (length/type; 0x0B + 4 = complete 15-byte frame)
  [3]     11 stable, 12 one-shot event/update
  [4]     state enum: 31, 22, 43, 33 observed
  [5]     bit 80 follows power
  [6]     setpoint in Celsius
  [9]     setpoint mirrored in rounded Fahrenheit
  [13]    opaque; 20 correlated with OFF in one controlled session
  [14]    checksum

Main field correlations:
  [3]     11 stable, 12 event/update
  [4]     state enum; panel 22 is normalized to main 21
  [5]     bit 80 follows power
  [6]     setpoint Celsius
  [8]     operation/status candidate (02 when powered in captured session)
  [11..12] opaque sensor/status values
  [14]    setpoint mirrored in rounded Fahrenheit
  [15]    constant 0A in all captures
  [21]    checksum

Captured action deltas:
  POWER ON: panel [5] 00 -> 80
  TEMP +18->19: [6] 12 -> 13, [9] 40 -> 42
  TEMP -19->18: reverse
  MODE cycle: [4] 31 -> 22 -> 43 -> 31
  FAN action in 31 state: [4] 31 <-> 33
  POWER OFF: [5] 80 -> 00 and one-session [13] 00 -> 20

The appliance manual has Cool, Fan and Dry modes. This makes 31/33=Cool,
22=Fan and 43=Dry a strong working map, but only the deltas above are direct
protocol facts. A later user-confirmed two-speed capture resolved low-nibble 3
as High speed in Cool; see isolated-features-20260812.txt.
The later isolated capture also resolved Timer as byte-5 bit 20. Sleep and
Swing remain unlabelled and are forwarded transparently.
